Voicely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Voicely is a local-first voice recording and transcription app. It is built so that your recordings and transcripts stay on your devices and in your personal iCloud account — nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

What data Voicely handles

  • Audio recordings you make in the app.
  • Transcripts of those recordings, including word-level timestamps.
  • Note metadata such as titles and creation dates.

Where processing happens

Speech-to-text transcription runs entirely on your device using an open-source speech model (WhisperKit). Your audio never leaves your device for transcription. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your notes and transcripts sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit and iCloud Drive). This data is stored under your Apple ID, governed by Apple's privacy terms, and is not accessible to us. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

Network access

Voicely connects to the network only to (a) download the speech model you choose (from the public Hugging Face model repository) and (b) sync via your iCloud account. No recording or transcript content is included in the model download request.

Microphone

Voicely requests microphone access solely to make the recordings you initiate. Recording only happens when you press record.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained only where you can see it: on your devices and in your iCloud account. Deleting a note in the app deletes its recording and transcript. Deleting the app (and its iCloud data via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud) removes everything.

Children

Voicely does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
