Voicely — Support

Voicely is a local-first voice recorder with on-device transcription. Everything is processed on your device; sync happens through your own iCloud account.

Getting started

  1. Download a speech model first. Open Settings inside the app and download a model (Small is a good start; larger models are more accurate but slower). This needs an internet connection once; transcription itself works offline afterwards.
  2. Allow microphone access when prompted, then press record. Transcription appears live, segment by segment, while you speak.
  3. Tap any word in a finished transcript to jump the audio to that moment; the current phrase highlights as playback advances.

Frequently asked questions

The transcript is empty or says "no speech detected"

Check that the correct microphone is selected in your system settings and that the input level moves when you speak. Voicely shows an orange “No microphone signal” banner while recording if the input is silent.

Transcription looks wrong or is in the wrong language

Try a larger model in Settings, or set the language explicitly instead of Auto. You can re-run any note with Re-transcribe after changing models.

Notes don't sync between devices

Sync requires being signed in to the same iCloud account on both devices, with iCloud Drive enabled. Text syncs first; audio may take longer to appear.

A long transcription was interrupted

Reopen the app — interrupted transcriptions resume automatically from where they stopped, keeping the transcript and its tap-to-seek timestamps intact.
